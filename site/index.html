How It Works

Secure by design. Simple by choice.

Three steps. That's it.
1 Plug into your router
2 Run the setup wizard
3 Your network is protected
Operational in under 15 seconds. No certifications required.
  • Tamper-proof operating system

    Read-only SquashFS root with dm-verity integrity checking on every boot. Malware cannot modify the OS, even with root access.

  • Automatic updates, zero risk

    A/B partition scheme with cryptographic signature verification. If an update fails, the device automatically rolls back. You're never left unprotected.

  • Your data stays home

    100% local processing. No cloud accounts, no telemetry, no data leaving your network. Your traffic is your business.

  • Encrypted DNS by default

    All DNS queries use DNS-over-TLS with DNSSEC validation. Your ISP cannot see or tamper with your DNS lookups.

  • Network segmentation

    Zone-based architecture with VLAN support. Isolate IoT, guests, and kids into separate networks so a compromised smart bulb can't reach your laptop.

FAQ

Common questions

What happens after the first year?

Your appliance continues to work as a firewall and VPN. The €99/year subscription keeps threat detection rules, DNS blocklists, and firmware updates current. Think of it like antivirus signatures — threats evolve, and your protection should too.

Do I need to replace my router?

No. ZenTI Unify sits between your router and your network. Your existing router handles Wi-Fi and internet connectivity. ZenTI handles security, filtering, and segmentation.

Why Suricata instead of a proprietary engine?

Suricata is the most widely deployed open-source IPS engine in the world, used by governments, ISPs, and enterprises. It's transparent, auditable, and backed by a global community. No black boxes.

Can my kids bypass the parental controls?

Controls are enforced at the network level via DNS filtering with forced DNS redirect. Devices cannot use alternative DNS servers. Schedules are checked every 60 seconds. Switching to mobile data is the only workaround.

Is my data sent to the cloud?

No. Everything is processed locally on the appliance. No cloud accounts, no telemetry, no analytics. Update checks contact our server only to compare version numbers.

What if an update bricks the device?

It can't. ZenTI uses A/B partition firmware with cryptographic verification. If an update fails integrity checks, the device boots the previous working version automatically.
